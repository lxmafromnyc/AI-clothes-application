      What Fynd reads and sends
      
                  
        
                • It reads a page only after you click the Fynd button in Chrome’s toolbar on it — or, on a site you chose to let Fynd read, as you move between its pages. It reads the product’s name, brand, sizes and size tables, never form fields or anything you type.
                  • 
        
                • Your references and how purchases fit are kept in this browser only (Chrome’s extension storage). They are never sent anywhere.
                  • 
        
                • The measurements Fynd uses for your reference ship with the extension. Looking them up sends nothing: the panel makes no network requests. “Open in Find Fit” opens Fynd’s site with the chart in the page address after #, which browsers don’t send to servers.
