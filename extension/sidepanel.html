            What Fynd reads and sends
            • It reads a page only after you press the Fynd button on it — or, if you allowed it, a page on a retailer listed here. It reads the product’s name, sizes and size tables, never form fields or anything you type.
            • Your references and how purchases fit are kept in this browser only (Chrome’s extension storage). They are never sent anywhere.
            • To find measurements, it may send Fynd’s server a product’s page address (on a supported retailer, or a link you typed), or a brand, kind of garment and size label. Nothing about you, and no other browsing, is sent.
