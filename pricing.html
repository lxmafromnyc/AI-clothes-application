The details

What you are actually buying

What counts as a live product search?

One request that reaches a real product source and comes back with listings. Browsing Discover, re-reading results you already have, and a search that finds nothing because nothing matched all cost you the one search that ran.

What counts as an AI token?

Reading your request into a set of constraints costs tokens, counted exactly as the model reports them. A typical request is a few hundred, so the free allowance is dozens of requests a day rather than a handful.

Where do I type my card?

On Stripe, not here. Fynd sends you to Stripe Checkout and never sees, handles or stores a card number. Changing a card, switching plan, downloading invoices and cancelling all happen in Stripe's billing portal, reached from your account page.

What happens if a payment fails?

Stripe retries it and tells Fynd. While the subscription is unpaid your allowance goes back to Free, and your account page says why and links to the portal to fix the card. Nothing is deleted; the plan comes back with the payment.

What happens when I cancel?

You keep the plan you paid for until the end of the period you paid for, and then go back to Free. Cancelling is a button in the billing portal.
