How the two meters work

What is a live search?

One request out to a real product source. It is the expensive part of FindWear, so it is the meter shown first — and it is only counted when the search actually runs. A search that fails before it reaches the source is not charged.

What are AI tokens?

What the model spends reading your request and turning it into something the catalogue can be matched against. A typical request costs a few hundred. They are counted separately from searches, so running out of one does not stop the other.

When do the meters reset?

Free resets every day at midnight UTC. Pro and Max reset on the first of each month, also UTC. Nothing carries over — each period starts at zero.

What happens when I run out?

The request is refused and the page tells you which allowance ran out, how much of it there was, and when it comes back. Nothing is charged for a refused request.

Does a double-click cost two searches?

No. Each submission carries its own identifier, so a repeat of the same search is recognised and answered from the first one's result without spending a second allowance.

Can I buy a plan yet?

Not yet. Plans, limits and metering are built and enforced; payment is not connected, so every account is currently on Free.
