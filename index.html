Enter the garment measurements from the product’s measurement chart.

Use the finished-garment measurements — what the garment itself measures, often called “product measurements” or “garment measurements”. Don’t use a body-size chart: it says who a size is made for, not how big the garment is.

Copy the numbers for one size. Enter the ones the chart gives and leave the rest blank — but the chest is needed for any comparison, and Fynd needs at least three measurements taken the same way as yours.

Measurement unit

Switching converts the numbers already entered, so they keep their meaning.

Usually “chest” or “½ chest” on the chart. Flat means half the way around.

Usually “shoulder” or “shoulder width”.

Usually “length” or “body length”. “Back length” is usually from the centre back of the neck.

Usually “sleeve”. A very long sleeve figure is often from the centre back of the neck.
