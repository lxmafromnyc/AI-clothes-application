    From a sentence to the exact piece.

    No filters, no categories. You say it; Fynd takes it apart into the things a search can use; real listings come back.

    1. 01 — YOU SAY IT

      “a linen shirt for a summer wedding, under $120”

      In your own words

      Describe it the way you would out loud. There is nothing to pick from a menu.

    2. 02 — FYND READS IT

      a linen shirt for a summer wedding, under $120

      Taken apart

      The garment, colour, fit, fabric, occasion and budget are read out of the sentence.

    3. 03 — FYND FINDS IT
      ShirtLinenWedding · SummerUnder $120

      Real pieces back

      Listings from real retailers, each with its price and a link to the retailer’s own page.
