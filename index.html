How Fynd works

  1. 1

    Paste a product link

    Any clothing product page. Fynd reads that page on its own server — never anything private, and never your profile.

  2. 2

    See its sizing chart

    The chart as the shop publishes it, with its units and source, and a note on what it measures: your body, the garment, or only size labels.

  3. 3

    Compare with what fits

    With a reference garment on your fit profile, Fynd sets its measurements beside the chart — only where the chart’s measurements can be checked against yours.
