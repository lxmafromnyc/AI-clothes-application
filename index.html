02 · The item you’re considering

Enter the finished-garment measurements from the retailer’s chart.

Use the finished-garment measurements — what the garment itself measures, often called “product measurements”. Not a body-size chart: that says who a size is made for, not how big the garment is.

Enter the numbers for one size, and leave blank any the chart doesn’t give. The chest is needed for any comparison, and Fynd needs at least three measurements taken the same way as yours.

cm

Usually “chest” or “½ chest” on the chart. Flat means straight across, half the way around; a chart that gives the whole way around says so.

cm

Usually “shoulder” or “shoulder width”: across the back, seam to seam, unless the chart says otherwise.

cm

Usually “length” or “body length”. “Back length” is usually measured from the centre back of the neck.

cm

Usually “sleeve”. A very long sleeve figure is often measured from the centre back of the neck, not the shoulder seam.
