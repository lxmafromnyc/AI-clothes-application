How Fynd works

  1. 1

    Choose your clothing

    Pick T-shirts, hoodies, jeans and more, or describe exactly what you want — colour, fit, price, occasion.

  2. 2

    Search across brands

    Fynd finds matching pieces from the brands and retailers its product search reaches, and links you to each one.

  3. 3

    Sized with your fit profile

    Saved a fit profile? Fynd uses the right part of it on its own — your T-shirts for a T-shirt search — and compares sizes only where a product’s measurements have been checked.
