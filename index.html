    How Fynd works

    1. 1

      Tell us what fits

      Pick a type of clothing, then one that already fits you, how you like it to fit, and what usually goes wrong. Four taps.

    2. 2

      Keep your fit profile

      Each type of clothing keeps its own answers, so a T-shirt size is never used for jeans. Add sizes by brand or measurements any time.

    3. 3

      Size suggestions, next

      Fynd is building size suggestions from your profile and each brand’s own verified size chart. Until a chart is checked, Fynd won’t guess.
