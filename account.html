How this works

Your account, plainly

Searching does not need an account

The Free allowance is counted per browser and comes back every day. An account is what a subscription belongs to, and what your usage is counted against once you have one.

Fynd never sees your Google password

Continue with Google sends you to Google's own pages. Google tells Fynd your email address and nothing else — no password, ever, and no access to your Google account beyond knowing who you are.

Why we confirm your email

Receipts, renewal notices and failed-payment warnings all go to your address, so we check it works before you can subscribe. The confirmation link expires and works once.

Your plan is decided on the server

What you are entitled to is read from your subscription in Stripe, not from anything this page holds. Editing the page changes what it draws and nothing about your account.
