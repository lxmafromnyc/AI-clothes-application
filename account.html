Billing

Where your card lives

Fynd never sees your card

Payment details are typed on Stripe's own pages and held by Stripe. Nothing on this site receives, handles or stores a card number, and no key that could charge one is ever sent to a browser.

Manage subscription opens Stripe

Changing a card, switching between Pro and Max, downloading invoices and cancelling all happen in Stripe's billing portal. Whatever you change there reaches Fynd as a webhook, and your plan here follows it.

Your plan is decided on the server

What you are entitled to is read from your subscription in Stripe, not from anything this page holds. That is why editing the page, or loading a success URL by hand, changes nothing about your allowance.
