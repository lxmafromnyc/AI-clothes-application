About Fynd

Clothes that match you, not the algorithm

Fynd is an AI clothing finder. You describe what you want to wear in your own words — it returns pieces that fit.

How the recommendations work

1

You describe what you want

One sentence in your own words — "a black shirt for school under $50" is plenty.

2

The AI reads your request

It works out the garment, colour, fit, occasion, budget and anything else you mentioned, then scores every piece against it.

3

You get a ranked shortlist

Strongest matches first, each with a one-line reason — so the list is quick to scan and easy to trust.

What it takes into account

What the AI looks for in your request, weighted by how much each usually changes what you'd actually buy.

Garment

What you actually asked for — a shirt, a hoodie, trousers.

Colour

The tones you name, mapped to the closest ones in stock.

Occasion

Work, school, evening, weekend — however you phrase it.

Fit

"Loose", "oversized", "fitted" all land on the right shape.

Budget

Say "under $50" and nothing above it appears.

Brands

Name a label and it gets a strong nudge up the list.

Is this a real shop?

No. Fynd is a demo experience and the catalogue is a small sample set, built to show how the recommendation flow works end to end.

Do I need an account?

No. Your request is used to rank the current shortlist and nothing else is stored.

Try it for yourself

A few taps is all it takes to see what Fynd picks for you.
