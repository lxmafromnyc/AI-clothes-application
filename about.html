About Fynd

Fynd finds the fit. Paste a link to any clothing product and it brings back that product’s sizing chart, says what the chart measures, and compares it with clothes you already own where it can.

Buying clothes online usually means opening a size guide, working out whether it measures your body or the garment, and guessing how that compares with what already fits you.

Fynd does that reading for you. Paste a product’s link and it reads the product’s own page — and the size guide it links to — on Fynd’s server, and shows the chart exactly as the shop publishes it: its sizes, its measurements, its units and its source.

It says plainly what it couldn’t find. A shop that draws its size guide with script, refuses automated readers, or publishes no measurements gets an honest answer and a link, never a chart from somewhere else. The search page is still there for describing what you want in plain words.

What Fynd understands

Garment

What you actually asked for — a shirt, a hoodie, trousers.

Colour

The tones you name, mapped to the closest ones available.

Occasion

Work, school, evening, weekend — however you phrase it.

Fit

“Loose”, “oversized” and “fitted” all land on the right shape.

Budget

Say “under $50” and nothing above it comes back.

Brands

Name a label and it is weighted up the list.

Questions

Are these real products?

When a product source is connected, yes — every item comes from that source and links to the retailer. When none is connected, Fynd shows a small sample catalogue and marks each row Sample so it cannot be mistaken for stock.

Do I need an account?

No. Pasting a link or searching never needs one; the link is used to read that page, and nothing about you is sent to the shop. A free account keeps an optional fit profile, which Fynd then uses to personalise sizing for the type of clothing you are shopping for.

What happens to a photo I attach?

Only its name, type and size are sent — never the file itself. Fynd does not read attachments yet, so they do not change your results.
