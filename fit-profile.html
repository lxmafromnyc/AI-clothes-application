    How your fit profile works

    Used when you shop

    Search for clothes as usual. When Fynd knows the type you are shopping for, it reads that type’s card — and only that one — to personalise sizing. Where a product’s measurements haven’t been checked, it says so rather than guess a size.

    Only you can see it

    It is kept with your account and read only when you are signed in. It is not shown on any other page, and nobody else can open it.

    We never guess your units

    Measurements are saved in the unit you choose. Fynd never reads inches as centimetres or the other way round — switching the unit converts what you typed, and says exactly what it changed.

    What we do not ask for

    No weight and no photos. Every measurement can be left out: the sizes you wear and the fit you like are a complete profile on their own.

    Deleting it

    Delete my fit profile removes it from your account straight away.
